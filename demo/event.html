About the Hackathon

HackVerse 2026 brings together over 300 passionate student developers, designers, and innovators across India. Over 24 continuous hours, teams will build cutting-edge prototypes tackling challenges in Healthcare, Autonomous AI, and Sustainable Energy.

Supported by MLRIT Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CIE), winning teams will receive incubation grants, cloud credits, and direct mentor support.

Official Rules & Guidelines

  • All source code, designs, and hardware prototypes must be created during the official 24-hour sprint.
  • Pre-existing commercial products or plagiarized code will result in immediate disqualification.
  • Open-source libraries, APIs, and frameworks are allowed with clear documentation.
  • Every team must submit a public GitHub repository and present a 3-minute live prototype demo.

Prizes & Rewards

  • 1st Prize: ₹25,000 Cash + Direct CIE Incubation Grant
  • 2nd Prize: ₹15,000 Cash + Cloud Credits
  • 3rd Prize: ₹10,000 Cash + Prototyping Mentorship
