Business Simulation Game The Equinox 2.0 Sub-Event

Startup Poly

A fast-paced business simulation game inspired by Monopoly where participants build startups, compete in dynamic markets, manage company finances, navigate risks, and make strategic decisions based on realistic business situations.

📅 30–31 October
📍 MLRIT Campus, Hyderabad
🏛️ Centre for Innovation & Entrepreneurship (CIE)

About Startup Poly

Startup Poly challenges participants to navigate the unpredictable journey of building and scaling a company. From managing initial working capital to acquiring key assets and mitigating market downturns, teams experience real entrepreneurial dynamics in an engaging Monopoly-style format.

Skills Developed

  • Financial literacy & cash flow management
  • Risk analysis and strategic business decision-making
  • Market competition and asset negotiation
  • Team collaboration under time pressure
