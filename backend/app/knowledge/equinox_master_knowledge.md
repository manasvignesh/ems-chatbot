## 1. Event Overview
- **Event Name**: The Equinox 2.0
- **Tagline**: Where Passion Meets Perseverance
- **Event Type**: 2-Day E-Summit
- **Dates**: 30–31 October
- **Location**: MLR Institute of Technology, Hyderabad
- **Description**: Equinox is a two-day E-Summit at MLR Institute of Technology, Hyderabad, designed as a vibrant and engaging environment where students come together to take on real-world challenges, explore entrepreneurship, learn, compete, connect, and gain practical and valuable experience.

## 2. Organizer — MLRIT CIE
- **Full Name**: Centre for Innovation & Entrepreneurship (CIE), MLR Institute of Technology
- **Established**: 2015
- **Mission & Focus**: MLRIT-CIE is an entrepreneurship development cell dedicated to nurturing young innovators and supporting early-stage startups. Its focus is on building a strong ecosystem that encourages growth, collaboration, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
- **Activities**:
  - Startup Cohorts: Incubation and mentorship support for young student entrepreneurs.
  - Workshops: Hands-on workshops in IoT, UI/UX, WordPress, and emerging technologies.
  - Microprojects: Interactive student-led product-development cohorts.
  - Hackathons: Conducts hackathons including MetaLoop (India's first AR/VR hackathon) and Inventron (flagship hackathon).

## 3. Sub-Events (The 10 Core Activities)

### 3.1 Spotlight
- **Description**: Features presentations from industry experts on technology, entrepreneurship, and startups.
- **Key Takeaways**: Students gain insights into emerging technologies, industry trends, and the future of entrepreneurship.

### 3.2 Crossroads
- **Description**: A business case-study competition where teams analyse real-world business challenges and develop practical strategies.
- **Skills**: Problem solving, decision making, and business skills.

### 3.3 Startup Expo
- **Description**: Provides startups with a platform to showcase their products, business ideas, and solutions.
- **Benefits**: Gives startups visibility and exposure to students while allowing students to discover new businesses and innovative ideas.

### 3.4 Brand Battles
- **Description**: A competitive debate between teams representing rival brands from the same sector.
- **Activities**: Participants defend their assigned brands using real-time examples, data, and case studies, challenging the strategies of opposing teams.

### 3.5 IPL Auction
- **Description**: A simulated cricket auction where participants take on the role of team owners, bid for players, manage budgets, build their own teams, and make strategic decisions.
- **Note**: This is an Equinox simulation event, distinct from real-world cricket match news or live scores.

### 3.6 Hustle Mania
- **Description**: A dynamic competition where students showcase marketing and negotiation skills by selling products of their choice and competing with others.
- **Skills**: Communication, persuasion, marketing, negotiation, and business acumen.

### 3.7 Internship Drive
- **Description**: Connects students with startups and companies offering internship opportunities.
- **Benefits**: Career exploration, practical experience, professional networking, and skill development.

### 3.8 Startup Poly
- **Description**: A fast-paced business simulation game inspired by Monopoly where participants build startups, compete in markets, manage finances, handle risks, and make strategic decisions.

### 3.9 E-Cell Meet
- **Description**: Brings together E-Cells from different colleges to connect, share ideas, exchange experiences, build relationships, collaborate, and explore cross-campus partnerships.

### 3.10 Pitch Deck
- **Description**: A startup pitching platform where students present ideas to investors, startup mentors, and industry experts for feedback, insights, and guidance.
